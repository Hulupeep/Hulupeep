## 🚀 About Me  

I’m a cross-disciplinary founder, product leader, and creative working at the intersection of education, AI, storytelling, and systems design. ### 👣 What I Do  
- ✍️ **Author of _Superpowered_** — a book for divergent minds learning how to build momentum and thrive in the age of AI  
- 🪜 **Founder of _The Ladder_** — a creative framework + community for making it easier to start, stick with, and ship your ideas  
- 📚 **Builder of _LearnCan_** — a curriculum-based learning engine for students and self-learners  
- 🧠 **Product consultant** — helping teams move from idea to execution with clarity and pace  
- 🎭 **Playwright** — my work has been produced off-Broadway, always rooted in emotional honesty and structure
